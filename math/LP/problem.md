# problem

## 1
求解下列线性规划问题：

$$
\begin{align}
\max \quad & z = 2x_{1} + 3x_{2} - 5x{3}\tag{1}\\
\text{s.t.}\quad &
\begin{cases}
x_{1} + x_{2} + x_{3} = 7\\
2x_{1} - 5 x_{2} + x_{3} \geq 10\\
x_{1} + 3 x_{2} + x_{3} \leq 12\\
x_{1}, x_{2}, x_{3} \geq0
\end{cases}\tag{2}
\end{align}
$$
